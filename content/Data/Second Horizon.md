# Second Horizon
_Masters of manipulation_
#### Info fragments
* People of the 2nd Horizon are called [[Santulan|Santulans]].
* Their home world is called **Argonnas**.
* Santulan society is governed by a kind of **Hive Mind** — called the **Symmetry** ("They who balance the universe"). Individuals are synchronised to a single will; a utopia bought at the cost of individuality, lived like a single enormous anthill.
* Santulans wield highly developed **psionic powers**. Their most notable capability is **Projection** — the ability to possess and remote-control a body in the Third Horizon from across the gap.
* The Second Horizon is at war with the [[First Horizon]].

### The Second Portal War
During the Second Portal War, the Santulans sent **Projections** (the Emissaries) into the Third Horizon with a dual purpose:
- Push back the Vestal invasion and keep the Third Horizon from falling into First-Horizon hands
- Assimilate the people of the Third Horizon — both as expansion and as a buffer zone protecting the Second Horizon itself

The **Projection channel was destroyed in the Portal War** and no new Projections have reached the Third Horizon since. All that remained was an **echo**: the first [[The Mystics|Mystics]] — people of the Third Horizon in whom mild psionic powers awakened as a side effect of the Projection work. These Mystics have **no active connection to the Santulans** and are not controlled from Argonnas.
