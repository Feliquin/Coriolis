# Santulian
_The First Horizon_

Scientist call the people from the First Horizons "Santulians". All humans have the same ancestry, but the Santulians developed very different to the people of the Third Horizon. They learned how to harvest and use the dark energy of the [[Darkness between the Stars]] to build powerful technology - but at a great price.
Their bodies are slender, their skin paler, and their limbs longer than that of people of the Third Horizon.

> [!TIP] Campaign notes
> - Found the dead body of a Santulian on board the [[Fazar]]
