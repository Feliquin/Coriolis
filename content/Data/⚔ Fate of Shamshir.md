THE MENG DELTA, [[Ordana]]
The region around Harmand's Monastery is dominated by mangrove swamps. It is also home to one of the [[Ayuin Cartel]]'s industrial islands, where, with the help of underpaid and overworked labourers, they produce large quantities of refined rubber. The area is under the protection of the Orcinus Wing, a deadly air company, which has a small airbase and radar station on a nearby isle. In addition, there is the rural community of Tempat, a gathering point for the local population but also a recruiting ground for the People’s Army of [[Ordana]].
However, the delta is notoriously difficult to navigate as it consists of shallow swamps, dense undergrowth, and inhospitable fauna. 
It is virtually impossible to cover any significant distance on 
foot. Instead its inhabitants use canoes, hydrocopters, or swamp crawlers.

THE CRYPTOSPHERE

living tissue. There is however no discernible way to disassemble or tamper with the sphere.

WELCOME TO ALTAI!
Khorsabad is one of the Third Horizon's most infamous settlements. The colony itself is located on the rocky dwarf planet Marakanda 6.5 AU from the star Altai.
Protected by the Black Sector's hidden weapon systems, Khorsabad is a sanctuary from the reach of the Zenitians. The Black Sector is a relic of the pre-Portal Wars, when the mighty rulers installed advanced weapons systems in the vast asteroid cloud that lies between the portal fields and most of the rest of 
the system. 
The area is filled with a large number of automatic torpedoes and warheads, as well as fortifications for soldiers. Only two routes exist today through the asteroid cloud: the original Yamuna Trail and the Tang Gol Path, which was created when the warlord Tang Gol managed to find an unguarded path in the asteroids during his invasion of Altai. Today, control of these flight paths is what has given Khorsabad much of its influence and wealth.
Altai is officially ruled by the Alabaster Council on Khorsabad, but an association of the most powerful families and corsairs controls much of the system. Different areas and neighbourhoods are ruled by different family groupings, and visitors generally need to buy protection from one of these to be guaranteed safe passage.

•  When the Kushtriya fled the Altai system following the 
rise of the warlord Tang Gol, they took with them much of their court, a vast amount of resources, and perhaps most spectacularly, the entire treasury of the Rajahdom. They departed in their flagship Shamshir.
•  Ibn was kidnapped upon return to Khorsabad and put in Annex A to help the Artabazus clan find the remains of the Kushtriya flagship, Shamshir.
•  Shamshir was the flagship of the Kushtriya and the hub of their military assets. In addition to its impressive armament, it is said to have contained a Command console for the

corsair defenses of the system, but the core of the Black Sector is apparently unchanged since the days of the Raj.

•  The Artabazus want to find this Command console, hoping that it will give them control over the entire black sector and thus be able to eliminate all the clan's opponents.
•  The result would most likely be a huge bloodbath, as Matriarch Genya Artabazus is totally ruthless. It is said that she does not even fear the Icons.
•  The Artabazus have forged close ties with the Consortium; thus, their regime would very likely sell out large parts of the system to the predatory practices of the Colonial Agency.
•  Because of this, Ibn has been very reluctant to assist Artabazus. However, he believes with the Cryptosphere 
found, they now have a potential clue to the whereabouts of Shamshir.
•  The Cryptosphere is rumored to contain some sort of connection to the Kushtriya dynasty but is guarded by an advanced biometric lock.
•  Ibn also tries to recruit the PCs to help him find and destroy Shamshir and its Command console, before the Artabazus clan gets their hands on it. In exchange, he promises that 
the PCs will receive 50 percent of Shamshir's cargo. If the stories are true, this is said to be several million birr worth of valuables and treasures.

SIVAS & KALA
The Sivas system is an old and archaic place. Ruled by the Jaghanir fleet of the Mogul clans, it remains as it was in the days of old. The majority of the system's settlements are located on the planet Sivas and its moon Karast. On Sivas there are a multitude 
of mining operations that hunt precious ores in the Grand Furrow, a titanic chasm in the planet's crust. The mining mostly benefits the Moguls who oversee much of the systems enterprise. On Karast, the conservative and esoteric brotherhood of the Lotus Brothers govern the moon with an ancient and iron fist. Nowhere 
in the system are Zenitian factions or enterprises welcomed. Few outsiders visit this backwater system, but those who do might find great riches in this ancient domain (for more information on Sivas see p.106 in the Coriolis Community Atlas).
The majority of this Act takes place around the gas giant Kala. The planet is on a destructive, meandering path towards annihilation into its sun. Among debris surrounding the gas giant lies the last materialistic remains of the Messenger's Seventh Herald, resting in a long forgotten temple on Meraj-7.
MERAJ-7
The gas giant Kala is slowly wandering towards Sivas’ sun. The 
cold gas giant is uninhabited. However, its strong gravity has endowed it with countless celestial bodies on its suicidal journey towards the sun. Surrounding it are trapped moons, asteroids, 
and other smaller celestial objects. Some have been mined ever since the first wave of settlers arrived on the Third Horizon. Today, however, the area is deserted as the ever deteriorating orbit of the planet has made them too dangerous to visit. The area is difficult to navigate and there are meteorite swarms in unstable orbits, some of which have battered Meraj-7 significantly.

THE DOOM OF KUSHTRIYA
THE ZHAU SYSTEM
The first thing one encounters when arriving in the remote Zhau system is the heavily fortified Lance Station. This mighty Portal station is controlled by the Honor Legion, an autonomous offshoot of the Legion. They have taken control of the trinary 
system and maintain their regime using the battleship Anabasis. The Honor Legion monitors the system's portal fields and all new ships entering the system are subjected to the Lance's sensor network. Travelers wishing to access the system's celestial bodies need to obtain a license, which can be arranged on the Lance. As much of the Zhau system is underdeveloped, the Honor Legion 
has little interest in developing the area itself. Instead, they are making a living by selling claims and settler licenses to colonists and entrepreneurs, often at high prices. Risk-takers who try to avoid this will soon experience the Honor Legion’s well-equipped arsenal up close. However, the Honor Legion has recently been infiltrated by agents and reporters from several factions, as the system's position has gained increased importance in the context of the blockade of Zalos by the Order of the Pariah (for more information on the Honor Legion and the Zhau system, see the Coriolis Community Atlas p.112).
ENTRY INTO THE HONOR LEGIONS DOMAIN
Upon arrival in the Zhau system, the PCs' ship is immediately subjected to a wide spectrum sensor scan from the Lance Station’s sensor network. They are then contacted by the Licensing and Transportation Agency who ask them to declare their intentions in the system (failure to comply with these directives causes the Anabasis to launch several nuclear torpedoes at the PCs’ ship after a sharp warning).
Regardless of whether they tell the truth or have a cover story, the PCs have to dock at the space station and head to the offices of the Licensing and Transportation Bureau for entry permits to the system. The space station is relatively busy, and there are Honor Legion personnel, often heavily armed, everywhere to monitor visitors and the ongoing commerce. As Zhau has many fortune seekers and settlers, there are a lot of hawkers at the station selling most of what a treasure seeker or future colonizer might need. However, prices are relatively steep due to the high fees charged by the Honor Legion.
The Licensing and Transportation Bureau lies in a run-down but rather grandiose section of the Lance. Inside the agency's office, 
the PCs are greeted by a dull official who explains the procedure for visitors. Entry into the system and Planet Z-9 will cost them around 2,000 birr. If the PCs are unable to pay, the Licensing and Transporting Agency can also accept payment in the form of raw materials, military equipment or technology (Ibn Tash may also 
be able to trade in some personal jewelry if the PCs run out of options).
HUNTED BY THE ARTABAZUS CLAN
In the course of the PCs' visit on the Lance Station, they attract the attention of one or more anonymous agents of the Altai corsairs. This gives the Artabazus clan information about their arrival in Zhau. The Arabazus clan is then able to piece together that the PCs and their ship are involved in Ibn Tash's search for Shamshir or at the very least that they harbour Ibn. This causes Genya Artabazus and her destroyer Asuai to depart for Zhau in search of the fugitive historian.
The journey from the Lance Station to the rocky planet Z-9 is 8 AD. About halfway into the journey, the PCs receive a system-wide broadcast originating from the Lance. The message is a warning that the destroyer Asuai, a Class IV corsair ship with 
its captain, Genya Artabazus has forced its way into the system and subsequently disappeared. The ship and its crew are to 
be regarded as hostile to the Honor Legion and thus the entire system.
ARRIVAL AT Z-9
Planet Z-9 is an inhospitable lump of rock halfway out of the system. The planet's landmass consists of three huge continents that are slowly being eroded by the surrounding acidic oceans. The geography consists of large mountain ranges, windswept coniferous forests and barren plains. Z-9 is notorious for the number of fortune seekers who have lost their lives to the 
planet's powerful earthquakes, dangerous weather conditions, and volcanic activity. Today, the planet's most famous spaceport, Akenatohna, has a reputation as a wild frontier town. The atmosphere is breathable, but experienced colonists always carry filtration masks and protective clothing as the planet is often plagued by storms with highly acidic rain.
The coordinates of Nova Solyma are located in the planet's southern hemisphere. The area is far from any settlements and is dominated by a cold and rugged mountain range and surrounding gravel fields.

THE OUTPOST
The coordinates lead to a gravel field near the foot of the 
mountain massif. A sensor scan of the area from orbit provides limited information, as the area is full of low-grade metal 
deposits. These are in too small concentrations to be lucrative 
to mine but create severe disturbances in sensor readings of the area. A couple of kilometers from the edge of the massif, near 
a small dried up river creek, are the remains of a building. It is 
a small structure, half sunk into the ground, bearing traces of long-term erosion from the planet's acid rain. The upper floor of the structure has collapsed but the lower part of the house is still intact. There is an entrance with a closed rusty metal door via a descending staircase in the ground. However, both the staircase and the door are blocked by fallen debris. It is however the best visible clue to the fate of Nova Solyma.
A.  Airlock: A small airlock. Its interior is worn and it has long since lost its hermetic function. The lock's machinery has long since rusted away, and on one wall someone has drawn a strange sign. With a successful roll on Culture, it is revealed to be the Hand of Nazar, an ancient symbol of protection against the Darkness between the Stars.
B.  Main room: As the doors slide open, the PCs are greeted 
by stale air wafting out. The room is plunged in darkness. What the PCs see when they look in is a common room with beautiful but flaking murals. In a few places there are more hand-painted symbols of protection. The floor has a beautiful geometric mosaic inlaid into it. What catches the visitors’ attention, however, is the large number of dried-up human skeletons scattered around the room. There are around 30 of them, all dressed in old-fashioned work clothes that are now in rags. The skeletons have long since been scraped clean of soft tissue. 
When the room is lit, a small army of unpleasant insects quickly scurries into cracks and crevices. On closer inspection, it appears that the remains have not been subjected to any violence. However, a successful roll on Medicurgy or the use of an environmental sensor informs the PC that all the bones throughout the building have a slight radioactive signature.
C.  Engine room: A small closet houses the controls for the building's life support system. These are destroyed beyond repair by the ravages of time.
D.  Cupboard: A larger wardrobe contains crumbling rags of 
work clothes and a couple of dusty protective masks.
E.  Office: An office that once must have belonged to a guard 
captain or foreman. The interior is worn and a large stone desk stands in the center of the room with a cracked plastic office chair next to it. Under the desk is a skeleton draped 
in rags of once fine clothing, holding a jeweled dagger. The rags are stained with black flakes of dried blood. At the skeleton's feet is an ancient tabula, covered in dust and probably untouched for centuries.
Decrypting the tabula requires an external power source 
and a success on a difficult roll on Technology or Data Djinn (-2). The tabula contains only an incoherent farewell letter penned by the dead noble, expressing profound sorrow 
over the death of all those around him at the hands of “the wrathful wraith of Shamshir’s heart”.
F.  Anteroom: The door is locked and has long been bolted 
shut. It takes a successful Force or Technology roll to open the door. Inside the room, the previous furniture has been carelessly pushed against the walls and the center of the room has been hastily turned into some kind of chapel. Eight Icons have been placed in a row, surrounded by a jumble 
of old offerings. The Icons appear to have been crudely assembled from various objects; the image of the Dancer 
is carved from a bar of soap, the Judge is made from a few pieces of wood bound together, and so on.
G.  Collapsed staircase: The stairs leading up towards the collapsed second floor are blocked by debris. No access is possible.
H.  Sanitation module: A small room containing a shower 
cubicle and toilet. On the floor are two more skeletons dressed in rags.
I.  The furrow: The smaller gully next to the building looks from the air to be the remains of a dried up watercourse. However, upon closer inspection of it, it is clear that these are the heavily eroded tracks of a larger land crawler of some 
kind (no roll required). The gully continues up towards the mountain massif. After a couple of kilometers, it disappears into a large cave in the rock face.
NOVA SOLYMA
After several kilometers, the ancient tracks lead to a large, deep cave in the wall of the mountain massif. The cave opening is about 30 meters long and 17 meters high. Inside the cave are a pair of rusted massive gates which stand wide open.
The entrance room behind the gates is an enormous chamber 
with marble pillars, and in the middle of the room is a large statue depicting the green spider seal of the Kushtriya. Beyond it a great
corridor similar to a boulevard leads into the mountain. Anyone 
is capable of discerning the grand design envisioned by the architect of this now ruined complex. The corridor goes straight into the mountain, and along it are rusted tools and vehicles used in construction. Everywhere in the half-finished ruins there are the beginnings of majestic frescos, pillars, and mosaics. The 
main corridor is intersected by smaller ones leading to several sub-assembled halls, half-finished residential areas and dried-
up fountains. Its design is clearly related to Marakanda and Khorsabad. A PC with an environmental sensor will pick up traces of radiation in the area. Everything is covered in darkness and dust.
While journeying in the ruins, the PCs discover the remains of the settlement's inhabitants as there are piles of mummified remains scattered all over the ruins. They seem to have died instantly, with some having fallen while running towards the exit. The bodies are in bad shape, no more than gnawed on skeletons with moth-eaten clothing. There are both nobles and workers among the dead based on their outfits.
The main corridor leads to the collapsed dome with the last resting place of Shamshir. It is here where the final confrontation of the Campaign occurs.
When the PCs enter Nova Solyma, the GM receives 3 Darkness Points.
EXPLORING NOVA SOLYMA
The PCs can leave the main corridor and explore the different districts of the settlement if they wish. Choose or randomly select one or more areas from the table to the right. There are no real items of interest or use among the ruins.
1 - BATHS: Unfinished basins and fountains dot the area. The sound of the PCs' feet echoes strangely among the beautiful mosaics and dusty fountains.
2 - COMMON HOUSES: Preassembled, boxlike structures dot 
the area, summoning associations to the servants’ quarters of Khorsabad. These frugal lodgings have many of their previous occupants still within, marking them as the final resting place of the majority of the settlement's inhabitants.
3 - SERVICE SECTOR: Rusted tubes and cracked machinery dominate the cave. Everything is rusted and defunct, as the shadows form strange shapes. Giant pipes go down into the 
floor, suggesting that the main power of the Nova Solyma was geothermal.
4 - NATURAL CAVE: This part of the cave system never received
any construction. The bare rock walls stick out with their coarseness. Small crevices and cracks shelter tiny, skittering creatures.
5 - SUNLESS GARDEN: A large open area, the roof covered with light panels and the floor with the remains of ancient hydroponic channels. Withered equipment suggests that this was a garden before the settlement perished.
6 - PUBLIC WORKS: Large, boastful structures in varying levels of assembly must have been meant for governmental agencies and other public functions. In the center of the area stand enormous statues of Kushtriya regents of old, gazing with unseeing eyes at those who trespass into the area.
FREE EPISODES AT NOVA SOLYMA
LAMKA NEST
Two Lamka Lizards (Core rulebook, p.323) emerge quickly from 
the darkness, hissing menacingly before attacking the PCs. A successful roll on Survival reveals that the lizards have a nest with eggs behind a pile of rubble close by, which they protect with their lives. Cost: 1 DP
EARTHQUAKE
A tremble in the rock soon grows to a potent earthquake that makes the cave walls shake like the walls of a paper bag. All PCs must make a successful roll on either Dexterity, Observation, or Survival to avoid suffering 1 point of Damage. The phenomena ends after a few seconds. Cost: 1 DP
THE IRRADIATED DEAD 
The PCs come across a closed gilded metal door among the passageways of the colony. They suddenly hear a slow, dull knocking behind it, which persists in the otherwise silent caverns. Opening the door requires a success roll on Force or Technology. 
If opened, a noxious waft of dead air escapes from within and reveals a small room piled with two dozen mummified colonists. The room is heavily radioactive and everyone present when the room opens suffer 1 point of Radiation and 1 point of Stress as the cause of the knocking is nowhere to be found. Cost: 1 DP
THE COLLAPSED DOME
An enormous open space reveals itself at the end of the main corridor. High above, a cracked, dark green glass dome forms a broken ceiling as large piles of rocks and glass litter the plaza-
like area.This was to be the centerpiece of the new city of the Kushtriya nobles and the resemblance to Khorsabad’s grandeur
is obvious. On the other side the entrance to a grand alabaster palace can be discerned, despite being mostly obscured by rocks. It is now only a facade, as the entire palace has caved in. The grand royal hall is now nothing but a tomb. On the left side of 
the dome a gigantic ship rests. Its intricate and gilded hull lies half-buried in jagged stones. Besides a yawning airlock the name of the ship, Shamshir, is painted in golden flourishes, and an enormous green spider like sigil is engraved in the hull. This is the last resting place of the once proud flagship of Marakanda.
THE FALL OF NOVA SOLYMA
NOVA SOLYMA WAS TURNED INTO A TOMB JUST A DECADE AFTER 
ITS FOUNDING. ACATASTROPHIC REACTOR ACCIDENT KILLED THE MAJORITY OF ITS INHABITANTS, LEAVING THE FEW SURVIVORS 
TO WASTE AWAY FROM RADIATION POISONING. THE CAUSE OF THE REACTOR ACCIDENT IS UNCLEAR, BUT A SERIES OF TECHNICAL FAILURES LED TO A MASSIVE OVERLOAD OF THE SHIP'S REACTOR. 
AS A RESULT, A HUGE CLOUD OF HIGHLY RADIOACTIVE STEAM WAS CREATED IN THE REACTOR THAT AUTOMATICALLY WAS VENTED 
TO AVOID CAUSING A CATACLYSMIC EXPLOSION. THE GAS CLOUD KILLED ALMOST EVERYONE IN THE SETTLEMENT AS IT FILLED THE PASSAGEWAYS WITHIN MINUTES. ONLY A FEW MANAGED TO ESCAPE AND MAKE THEIR WAY TO THE OUTPOST, WHERE THEY PERISHED FROM RADIATION, THIRST, AND THE HARSH CLIMATE.
SHAMSHIR
Shamshir is buried under 100 000s of tons of rock. The Kushtriya hid their proud battleship by covering it with a giant green glass dome, in turn covered with immense amounts of rubble caused by a series of well-structured detonations in the mountainside above. The result was a well-hidden pocket with the entire ship intact inside, serving as a keepsake of their lost kingdom. This also swept away any outward traces of Nova Solyma, which became an entirely hidden cave complex.
Unfortunately, the centuries have not been kind to the Kushtriya site's construction, and after the fatal reactor accident, the dome has gradually collapsed due to the area's seismic activities. Shamshir now lies half-crushed (represented by red areas on the map) and the areas that still have structural integrity are heavily radioactive.
Five areas remain: the Forward Weapons Battery, the Command Bridge (including the Command console), the Officer's Corridor, the Treasury, and the Reactor. One of the ship's airlocks (south of the Front gun battery) connects it to the cave halls of Nova Solyma.
1. The bridge: Here, the greatness of the Kushtriya can still 
be sensed. The ship has no power, but a successful roll on Data djinn allows the last logs from the ship to be retrieved, telling the story of the reactor accident (see The fall of Nova Solyma above).
The Command console is located on the bridge. It is a large, black cubical machine the size of a man, integrated into the ship with several thick cables. At its front is a black screen. 
It is currently without power and its content is heavily encrypted, meaning that accessing it would take several days. Detaching it from the ship requires a successful roll on Technology, and carrying it off the ship requires some sort of transportation. If restored to its working state it generates 
a complicated program that tracks all the different defence systems, traps and countermeasures of the Black Sector.
The Command console can be destroyed with a successful roll on Technology. It can also be destroyed with explosives 
or heavy gun fire, but such methods risk creating a catastrophic collapse of the bridge.
•  2. The officers’ corridor: The officers' corridor is where the most senior members of the Kushtriyan navy were housed during the war. The corridor is partially destroyed, but some cabins are still intact. There is quite a bit of radiation here and PCs suffer 1 Radiation point per hour while in this area. Any PCs exploring the cabins can roll on Observation, a successful roll means a found item. Choose or select one randomly from the item table below (for specific bonuses 
and rules for the items, please see the Equipment chapter in the Core rulebook).
•  3. Front gun battery: Formerly home to huge war machines, mighty plasma cannons, and meson batteries, this area is mostly empty as the explosives have been removed. There are the dusty remains of some of the workers scattered around the room as well as one ancient torpedo still stuck 
in the torpedo-tube, which can be detonated or have its payload removed with a successful roll on Technology or Ranged Combat (symbolising the PC’s capacity to dismantle a gun rather than shooting it).
•  4. The reactor: The reactor on the battleship used to be a marvel of technology, but it is now a rusting, decaying ruin bathed in radiation. Staying here for long is fraught with mortal danger. PCs staying here suffer 2 (!) Radiation points per minute!
If a PC wishes, it is very easy to create a timed and extremely destructive reactor detonation with a successful roll on 
Technology or Data djinn (+2 dice). Such a detonation would turn all of Shamshir and Nova Solyma to molten rubble and cause large parts of the mountain above to cave in and crush the entire colony.
•  5. The Treasury: A sturdy locked arched door, beautifully decorated with reliefs, protects the treasury. Entering it requires a code and passing a biometric DNA lock. The DNA lock can be opened by using Shirin's remains, but cracking the code requires a difficult roll on Data djinn (-3 dice) and takes almost an hour. Anyone interacting with the door also suffers 1 point of Radiation per minute.
Inside the treasury, an amazing but deadly sight awaits. Although much of the treasure has been used to fund the colony, the room is full of wondrous heirlooms and riches. Golden tapestries, bejeweled statues, silk robes worthy of Dabaranian princes, and several stacks of ingots made 
from precious metals line the walls. The value of the 
entire hoard is more than a dozen millions worth of birr. However, everything is highly radioactive, and staying in the room generates 1 point of Radiation per minute. Bringing something from the room will expose everyone in proximity to the treasure to 1 point of Radiation per hour.
THE FINAL CONFRONTATION WITH 
THE CORSAIR MATRIARCH
If the PCs have some sort of look-out left outside of the cave when venturing inside, they report that an enormous corsair spaceship arrives above the mountain range and that several small vehicles are fast approaching the cave entrance around the time the PCs explore Nova Solyma. (The Asuai has the characteristics of a Destroyer including its crew found on p.161 in the Core rulebook)
Around the time when the PCs gain access to Shamshir, or when 
it is narratively convenient, Genya and her crew enter the caves 
on several small grav bikes. In total there are 8 corsairs and 
Genya racing to the collapsed dome and trying to gain access to Shamshir.
When they encounter the PCs, Genya either tries and reason with them to give up the Command console or attacks mercilessly depending on the attitude of the PCs. Genya has no interest in 
the treasure aboard the ship and gladly offers the PCs it in return for the Command Console. If Ibn is present, he never accepts 
an agreement with Genya, preferring to blow up the whole cave rather than handing over the Command console to her.
If a fight ensues, Genya orders her corsairs to attack, but hangs 
back by the grav bikes herself. If the battle goes in the PCs favor, she tries to escape on one of the grav bikes back to her ship.
THE ARRIVAL OF THE HONOR LEGION
At a narratively fitting time the Honor Legion attacks the Asuai while it is stationary above the mountain. They have hunted the corsairs through the system and wish to see these arrogant intruders destroyed. The Honor Legion has three Class III warships that assault the massive corsair ship with their batteries of torpedoes and accelerator cannons. The ensuring battle tears the sky asunder with fiery explosions.
If the PCs join the fight with a ship of their own, the battle will swiftly be over and Asuai will crash into the barren planet in 
a cataclysmic inferno. Thus ends the dream of a new raj of Khorsabad.
Otherwise, the Asuai destroys one of the Honor Legions ships before fleeing, badly damaged, towards the outer rims of the star system, regard-less whether their Matriarch is onboard or not.
ENDINGS
Depending on how the PCs handled the final confrontation in 
Nova Solyma, there are a few different possible outcomes of the campaign.
•  If the PCs blew up Shamshir along with the Command console, Ibn is most pleased, and rewards them with whatever sum they agreed on earlier plus an extra 10.000 birr. The PCs Patron also writes off a total of six segments worth of debt for helping his business associate. If Ibn has been killed during the campaign, the PCs are only rewarded with the reduced debt.
•  If the PCs defeat Genya Artabazus, they are later contacted 
by Abdallah Al Tamir who congratulates them on taking 
out one of Varanasi Collectives' rivals. As a token of their appreciation, they provide protection for the PCs whenever they are in the Altai system.
•  If Genya Artabazus flees, she has a burning desire for vengeance. She and her corsairs will hunt the PCs in the future, meaning that the Quadrant of the Pillar will be a much more dangerous place for them from now on.
•  If Genya manages to claim the Command console, she will return to Altai and use it to deactivate the Black Sector, leaving Altai and Khorsabad defenseless against a massive attack by the Consortium and the Legion. In return the Artabazus clan will take control of the Alabaster Council 
and Genya proclaims herself as the Rajh of Khorsabad. The spiraling conflict will expand and result in a lengthy and bloody war that will spread across the entire Quadrant.
•  If the PCs barter with the Command console or form an alliance with Genya, she will give them a seat at the reformed Alabaster Council. This position will grant them some influence in the Altai system but also give them many new enemies. Combined with the Quadrant-wide war, the future will indeed be a dangerous one for our protagonists.
