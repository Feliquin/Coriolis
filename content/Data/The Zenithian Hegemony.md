# The Zenithian Hegemony
_Zenithians_

The aristocrats of the Zenithian Hegemony were the elite of the Monolith on the planet [[Kua]]. Their role in the Second Portal War and the cataclysm initiated by the fall of Coriolis, brought them to the brink of exstinction. Only very few members of the Hegemony survived. 

Alignment: Zenithian 
Allies: [[The Consortium]]
Opponents: The Order of Pariah 
Influence: Astûrban (Secret police)
Territory: Monolith on [[Kua]]  
VIP: Lian Quassar (Leader), 
Olandria Quassar (Council member), 
Igritte Arianites (Head of the Astûrban)

Members of the Zenith captain’s family and other high-ranking officers of the crew of 
the Zenith founded the Hegemony after the schism onboard. The Hegemony opposed [[The Consortium]] for many decades, but after the destruction of the Second Portal War they needed [[The Consortium]] for protection. 

For many years, members of the Hegemony served the Cooperations as influencers and poster childs. [[The Consortium]] used the noble names for advertisement and negotiations with border systems. But the Hegemony’s hidden wealth and persistancy finally brought them back to power.

Now, Lord Lian Quassar officially rules the faction. He still has control over the infamous secret police, the Astûrban. Meanwhile, his sister Olandria Quassar sits in the Alabaster Council.
