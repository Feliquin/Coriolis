# The Legion
_Zenithians_

The mercenary navy financed and hired by [[The Consortium]]. The Legion is comprised of a diverse collection of mercenaries and armed skippers, gathered around the remnants of a fleet squadron from the Portal War.

Alignment: Zenithian
Allies: [[The Consortium]]
Opponents: [[The Draconites]] 
Influence: The Legionaires, The Legion navy 
Territory: [[Odacon]], [[Kua]], and [[Hamura]]
VIP: Sahumia Taslim (Council member), 
Kastar Palu (Steel Skull Commander), 
Hulia Illios (General of the fleet)

The legion has close ties to [[The Consortium]] -  some even say that they might as well just 
join [[The Consortium]], but this would not be in the corporations’ best interest, as it would mean one less Zenithian vote on the Council.

The Legion’s fleet suffered great losses during the Second Portal War, but [[The Consortium]] financed the built of several new cruisers, torpedo ships, and two new flagships (named Shahrazad and Dunyazad to honour the two flag ships lost in the final battle of the war).

New ships with jump capability have joined the Fleet in the last few years. [[The Consortium]] uses them to quickly gain influence over the newly discovered systems outside the portal routes.
