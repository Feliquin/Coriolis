# Vestal
_The First Horizon_

Scientist call the people from the [[First Horizon]] _Vestals_. All humans have the same ancestry, but the Vestals developed very different to the people of the [[Third Horizon]]. They learned how to harvest and use the dark energy of the [[Darkness between the Stars]] to build powerful technology - but at a great price.
Their bodies are slender, their skin paler, and their limbs longer than that of people of the [[Third Horizon]].

Vestal civilisation is ruled by the **Immortal Emperor** (_Unsterblicher Kaiser_) from Al-Ardha. Their fleets rely on the feared **Butterfly Ships** (_Schmetterlingsschiffe_) and Distortion Torpedoes.

### The Second Portal War
During the Second Portal War the Vestals launched a **military campaign to conquer the Third Horizon**. The campaign failed. In its final phase, the **last known portal between the First and Third Horizon was destroyed**, cutting the two Horizons off from each other.
