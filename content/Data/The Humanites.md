# The Humanites
_Independant_

Once called “Semi-Intellects” or even more derogatory “Vermin”. Humanites are civilized humanoid species native to the Third Horizon. 

Alignment: Firstcome
Allies: [[The Draconites]]
Opponents: [[The Consortium]]
Influence: Free Trade, Nomad navy
Territory: Reserves on [[Kua]] and other planets. VIP: Phyiliias (Council Member),
Shikra (Leader of the [[Kua]] Nektara)

Humanites have been slaves since the Zenith arrived and were forced to help built Coriolis station. Later, slavery was briefly outlawed, but never completely eradicated. The “Humanite Act” during the 2nd War allowed hunt and capture of Humanites to help the war effords. After the war, most of the slaves were set free. Now, many Humanites live isolated within their clans and families in reserves, but some venture the stars. 

There are about 20 different humanite species, but the largest groups are:
* Nektara: Hunters with large canines and covered in fur. Slightly larger than the average human and fierce warriors. They originate from [[Kua]].
* Sirb: Feathered humanoids originated from the [[Algol]] Route. They have delicate bones with limited flight capabilities. Their natural tongue consists of chirp and click sounds, but they can speak human languages with some training.
* Ichtar: Ichtar originate from [[Kua]], where they sail the oceans on large barges. On first look they may appear as chubby short humans, with a blueish skin and high pitched voices.
* Bwaps: Reptilian humanoids that need a high humidity to exist. Origin unknown.
* Skavarra: Badger-like humanoids with unknown origin.

Humanites have one seat in the Alabaster Council and operate a separate Humanite Council.
