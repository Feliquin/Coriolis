# Ahlam's Temple
_Firstcome_

Once known as systematic explorers of the mysteries of experience and the senses, Ahlam’s Temple now is a full grown criminal organization and rival to [[The Syndicate]]. 

Alignment: Firstcome
Allies: [[The Free League]]
Opponents: [[The Syndicate]]
Influence: Courtesans, gambling, pleasure houses
Territory: [[Algol]], Border systems  
VIP: Sharim al-Farhat (gambling), 
Jasmina al-Farhat (courtesan services),
John Jarquisor (logistics)

Ahlam’s Temple started as a cult, but instead of the religious traditions of the Church, the Temple’s foundation was the eternal importance of the present. After the fall of Coriolis, more business oriented leaders took over and formed Ahlam’s Temple to the faction it is today: far removed from religious aspects and only dedicated to the pleasures of mind and body. And the accumulation of wealth, of course.

Members of the al-Farhat crime family from [[Algol]] saw the opportunities the Temple offered. Now, the Temple is basically in al-Farhat hands, with secondary positions given to friendly crime clans in the border systems.

The Temple’s tea and bathing houses are well known across the Horizon, as are their gambling casinos. They do not have a seat in the Alabaster Council.
