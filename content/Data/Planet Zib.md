# Planet Zib
_Cold planet with few settlements_

Celestial body in the [[Zib]] system.
The planet Zib has several large settlements, but is still far from being an established colony. The largest of these settlements is **Gahatea**, which also boasts the planet's most advanced research facility, the Astrolab. While the rest of the population has to make do with relative poverty and primitive technology, the inhabitants of Gahatea enjoy a stable network of information channels and advanced infrastructure. The **Astronomical Institute** in the colony on Zib is a place often used to train astronomers across the horizon. Although the Astrolab does not provide the most advanced technology, it offers the most affordable prices for training. Otherwise, the laboratory's main function is to map the stellar constellations in this area of the horizon and identify traces of other habitable systems. 
Of particular interest is a nearby newly formed star that may be reachable with the new jump drive technology. 
In orbit around Zib is a small and already outdated space station run by the brothers Hamsa and Yurbul.
