# Experience and Training

At certain points in the story, the GM will allow the group to spend Experience Points, usually after some major story event, and when the characters have free time for training.
## GAINING EXPERIENCE POINTS
After each session, all Travellers will gain 1 automatic experience point. Great roleplay and completed story arcs will earn them additional points (GM descretion).
## GAINING SKILLS
* New Skill: To gain a skill at level 0 costs 1 Experience point. 
* Training existing [[Skills]]: Gaining level 1 from level 0 costs 1. After that the cost is doubled, as shown on the following table. Each skill level must be bought in turn. The cost is reduced if the skill has been used since the last training point. Mark the checkbox near a skill if you either succeeded in a check with effect 5+ or failed a check. Only one mark can be made per skill.

## INCREASING CHARACTERISTICS
Physical and mental [[Characteristics]] may also be increased by appropriate activity. Gym work, sport and general exertion are good for physical [[Characteristics]] (STR, END and DEX) while study and problem-solving can be used to train the mind (INT and EDU). SOC cannot be trained; a Traveller who wants to improve his SOC will have to do it in-game and spend a lot of Birr in the process. LCK can not be raised with training.
