# Santulan
_The Second Horizon_

Scientist call the people from the [[Second Horizon]] _Santulans_. All humans have the same ancestry, but the Santulans developed very different to the people of the [[Third Horizon]]. They learned how to perfect the power of the mind and developed psi capabilities.
Their bodies are not that different from people of the [[Third Horizon]] - but with bronze colored skin and slightly enlarged foreheads.

### Society — the Symmetry
Santulan society functions as a kind of **Hive Mind**, governed by the **Symmetry** ("They who balance the universe"). All Santulans are synchronised to a single will — a civilisation lived like a single enormous anthill. The result is a utopia of harmony and efficiency, bought at the cost of individuality.

### The Second portal war
The Santulans sent 5 emissaries to the [[Third Horizon]], even if their goals are not that clear. Some say to warn and protect the people of the [[Third Horizon]]. Others insist that their plan was to weaken and corrupt the [[Third Horizon]] in order to assimilate it as a buffer zone for Argonnas. Either way, these emissaries were mere **projections** — and they invaded the minds of some scientists on Xene. With the new bodies they spread out into the [[Third Horizon]].

The powerful projection (crafted using an ancient mystic power called resonance) created a side effect: the Awakened or [[The Mystics|Mystics]] — random people in the Third Horizon in whom minor psi powers awakened. **The Projection channel was destroyed during the Portal War** — the Santulans can no longer reach the Third Horizon this way. The psi powers of the Mystics stayed as an echo of the channel, but Mystics have **no active connection to Argonnas** and are not controlled from the Second Horizon.

### Argonnas
The golden world of the [[Second Horizon]] is called Argonnas. It is the seat of the Symmetry.
