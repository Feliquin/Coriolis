# Santulan
_The Second Horizon_

Scientist call the people from the [[Second Horizon]] _Santulans_. All humans have the same ancestry, but the Santulans developed very different to the people of the [[Third Horizon]]. They learned how to perfect the power of the mind and developed psi capabilities.
Their bodies are not that different from people of the [[Third Horizon]] - but with bronze colored skin and slightly enlarged foreheads.
### The Second portal war
The Santulans sent 5 emissaries to the [[Third Horizon]], even if their goals are not that clear. Some say to warn and protect the people of the [[Third Horizon]]. Others insist that their plan was to weaken and corrupt the [[Third Horizon]]. Either way, these emissaries were mere projections - and they invaded the minds of some scientists on Xene. With the new bodies they spread out into the [[Third Horizon]].
The powerful projection (crafted using an ancient mystic power called resonance) created a side effect: the Awakened or Mystics, random people developing minor psi powers. Even after closing the connection to the [[Second Horizon]], psi powers stayed.
### Argonnas
The golden world of the [[Second Horizon]] is called Argonnas. It is controlled by the Symmetry - “They who balance the universe”.
