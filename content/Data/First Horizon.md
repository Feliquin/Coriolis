# First Horizon
_Masters of the Darkness_
#### Info fragments
* People of the 1st Horizon are called [[Vestal|Vestals]].
* Their home world is called **Al-Ardha** (or Earth in their own language) — the cradle of humanity.
* The First Horizon is ruled by the **Immortal Emperor** (_Unsterblicher Kaiser_).
* Vestal civilisation runs on the [[Darkness between the Stars|Darkness between the Stars]] — their technology, ships and artefacts consume Darkness instead of conventional energy.
* Their best-known warships are the **Butterfly Ships** (_Schmetterlingsschiffe_), armed with Distortion Torpedoes.
* The First Horizon is at war with the [[Second Horizon]].

### The Second Portal War
During the Second Portal War, the Vestals attempted a **military conquest of the Third Horizon**. The campaign ultimately failed. In its final phase, the **last known portal between the First and Third Horizon was destroyed**, cutting both sides off from each other. Any Vestals still on this side of the gap have been stranded ever since.
