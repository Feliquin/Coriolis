# Ibn Tash
_Trader of historical artifacts_

> [!TIP] Campaign notes
> - Wants to buy an ancient Cryptosphere from the head nun of the monastery in te Meng Delta.
> - Has lost most of his wealth and hopes that the Cryptossphere will set things right.
> - Works with the Varanasi Collective (a group of free traders or a wanted corsair group - depending on who you ask), [[Abba Al-Tamir]] is his daughter and part of the Varanasi Collective.
> - Left for Khorsabad in [[Altai]], where his store is located.
