_One of the Zenithian splinter factions_

> [!TIP] Campaign notes
> - Based in Ayuin, the capital on [[Planet Ordana]].
> - Controls the rubber production.
> - Known for brutal working conditions of the rubber workers.
> - Fights with the [[Ordana]] Freedom Army.
