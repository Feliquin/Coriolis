# The Mystics
_Independant_

Gifted with mental powers by the arrival of the Emissaries, the Mystics were demonized both 
by [[The Order of the Pariah]] and the Zenithian factions. Even [[The Church of the Icons]] saw them as a mockery to the Icons. Now the Mystics live secluded in remote monastaries to train their mental abilities.

Alignment: Firstcome
Allies: Ahlam’s Temple, [[The Draconites]] Opponents: [[The Order of the Pariah]]
Influence: Mystic powers
Territory: [[Algol]], [[Altai]] 
VIP: Tarek al-Farhat (Council Member),
Vinkor Kuma (Head Principal of the Academy)

The First Horizon agents did their best during the Second Portal War to put oil into the fire of hate against the Mystics to weaken the position of the Second Horizon. The result is called the “Mysticides” and it led to hundreds of killed Mystics all across the Horizon. 

Then came the day of the Fall, when the Mystic cult “Children of the Song” activated the Monolith on [[Kua]] and thus destroyed Coriolis station. Regardless of being mind controlled, many still blaim or outright hate the Mystics for that day.

In the years after, Mystics lived in hiding, avoiding showing their powers in public. Then the first Mystic Academy was founded and offered a secure home. 

In the passing decades, many Mystics trained in the academies ventured out into the Horizon. Met with prejudice they try to help the people of the Horizon and to clear their name from the crimes of the Children of the Song.

The Mystics have a seat in the Alabaster Council.
