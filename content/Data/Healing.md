# Healing

## UNCONSCIOUSNESS
Time: 1 minute
Effect: Average (8+) END check. A cumulative DM+1 applies for each roll after the first. If successful consciousness is recovered (setting END to 1 ). 
## FIRST AID
Requirements: Must be initiated within one minute of the injury occurring.
Time: 1D-2 rounds (min. 1 round)
Skill: Average (8+) Medicurgy check (EDU). 
Effect:The patient regains lost characteristic points equal to the Effect. Split between attributes as desired. Can be supported with Medkits/Trauma Kits and/or M-doses/T-doses. Can be self applied if not unconscious. Only once after receiving an injury.
## MEDICAL CARE
Requirements: A hospital or medical bay. Full bed rest. 
Only 1 or 2 Physical [[Characteristics]] can be damaged. 
Time: 1 day
Skill: no check needed
Effect: Heal physical Characteristic points equal to: 3 + END DM + Doctor’s Medicurgy DM, divided evenly between damaged physical [[Characteristics]]. Medkits/Traumkits/m-doses/t-doses can be used.
## SURGERY
Requirements: Requires a hospital or medical bay.
Time: 1D hours
Skill: Average 8+ Medicurgy (EDU).
Effect: Heal physical Characteristic points equal to: Medicurgy check Effect +3, split between attributes as desired. Medkits/ Traumkits/m-doses/t-doses can be used. Failure causes further injury equal to the Effect.
## NATURAL HEALING
Requirements: Full Rest.
Time: 1 day
Effect: Heal physical Characteristic points equal to:
1D + END DM divided evenly between damaged physical [[Characteristics]]. If three [[Characteristics]] are damaged this healing is reduced to only the END DM (may result in further damage).
## MENTAL CHARACTERISTIC HEALING
Time: 1 day
Effect: Heal one point per Mental Characteristic.
## AUGMENTATION
Augmented Travellers in low-tech hospitals suffer a penalty to Medicurgy checks equal to the difference between tech levels.
